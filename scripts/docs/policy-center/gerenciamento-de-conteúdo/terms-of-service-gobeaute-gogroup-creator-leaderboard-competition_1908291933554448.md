# #1 Lescent Leaderboard Participants

(EN)  

1. **Activation Overview**

The Lescent Creator Leaderboard Competition ("Activation") is a promotional campaign designed to encourage creators to promote Lescent products through TikTok Shop. The competition will rank creators based on their Gross Merchandise Value (GMV) generated during the Activation Period within a specific PID pool.  

2. **Activation Period**

The Activation will run from **March 5, 2026 to March 25, 2026 (10 days)**. All eligible GMV must be generated (published) within this timeframe to be counted toward the leaderboard ranking.  

3. **Eligibility Requirements**

To participate in the Activation, creators must meet the following eligibility criteria:  
**3.1 Basic Requirements**

* Creator must be based in Brazil
* Creator must be 18 years of age or older
* Creator must have a TikTok account with at least 2,000 followers and e-commerce permissions enabled
* **3.2 Minimum Activity Requirements**

To qualify for Leaderboard rewards, creators must achieve:  

* Minimum of 1 video OR 1 live on TikTok Shop (with product linked), featuring Lescent products published during the Activation Period

4. **Ranking Criteria**

**4.1 Primary Ranking Metric**Creators will be ranked based on their final total GMV generated during the Activation Period. The GMV calculation includes:  

* Live GMV: All paid orders generated during LIVE sessions (with product linked)
* Video GMV: All paid orders generated from shoppable videos (with product linked)
* Eligible Products GMV: Only GMV generated from the Lescent product line will be counted

**4.2 GMV Calculation Rules**

* Only GMV generated after registration will be counted
* GMV is calculated based on Order Accumulated GMV, not Settled GMV
* There is no restriction on publishing time - all eligible content published during the Activation Period will be counted

5. **Reward Structure**

**5.1 Reward Tiers**

|  |  |  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Tier** | **Ranking Position** | **Number of Winners** | **Reward (R$)** | **Total (R$)** |
| 1 | 1st Place | 1 | 4,000 | 4,000 |
| 2 | 2nd–4th Places | 3 | 2,500 | 7,500 |
| 3 | 5th–8th Places | 4 | 1,750 | 7,000 |
| 4 | 9th–14th Places | 6 | 1,083 | 6,500 |
| Total | 14 winners | 14 | - | **25,000** |

**5.2 Results**

* Maximum number of winners: 50 creators
* Total reward pool: R$5,200 (USD $1,000)

6. **Activation Rules**

**6.1 Content Requirements**

* Creators must use TikTok Shop features according to Activation Rules during the Activation Period
* All content must comply with TikTok Shop's Community Guidelines and Commerce Policies
* Content must prominently feature Lescent products

**6.2 Prohibited Activities**Creators who engage in any of the following will be disqualified:  

* Violation of TikTok Policies or these Terms
* Misleading Functionality and Effect claims
* Inconsistent Product Promotion
* Misleading Price representation
* False promises
* Pirated Content
* Vulgar Behavior
* Counterfeit or Knockoff products

7. **Leaderboard Display**

**7.1 Accuracy Disclaimer**The leaderboard display may not be accurate or in real-time. TikTok reserves the right to alter the leaderboard display due to technical issues or if there has been unsportsmanlike or other behavior prohibited by these Terms.  
**7.2 Winner Verification**A creator is not a winner even if the leaderboard indicates such position unless and until TikTok has:  

1. Verified the winner's total revenue
2. Officially announced such creator as a winner
3. **Reward Distribution**

**8.1 Payment Timeline**

* Winners will be verified within 10 business days after the Activation ends
* Rewards will be distributed within 40 days of winner verification
* All rewards will be paid in Brazilian Real (R$)

**8.2 Tax Obligations**Winners are responsible for any applicable taxes associated with their rewards. TikTok will issue necessary tax documentation as required by Brazilian law.  

4. **General Terms**

**9.1 Modification Rights**TikTok reserves the right to modify, suspend, or terminate the Activation at any time for any reason, including but not limited to technical difficulties, fraud, or violation of these Terms.  
**9.2 Dispute Resolution**Any disputes arising from this Activation will be resolved according to TikTok's standard dispute resolution procedures and Brazilian law.  
**9.3 Acknowledgment**By participating in this Activation, creators acknowledge that they have read, understood, and agree to be bound by these Terms of Service.
