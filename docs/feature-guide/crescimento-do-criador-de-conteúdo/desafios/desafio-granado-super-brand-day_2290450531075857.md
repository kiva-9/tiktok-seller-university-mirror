## Rewards Description

Contestants with the highest cumulative GMV during the Challenge Period will receive rewards based on their final ranking as follows:  

|  |  |  |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Tiers** | **Number of Winners** | **Reward** |
| **Tier 1: 1st Place** | 1 | R$0 |
| **Tier 2: 2nd Place** | 1 | R$0 |
| **Tier 3: 3th Place** | 1 | R$0 |
| **Tier 4: 4th Place** | 1 | R$0 |

## Requirements

1. Create content with Granado products available in the Super Brand Day promotion
2. The ranking is based on your live stream GMV (Gross Merchandise Value) ranking for the content related to Granado products during the campaign period.
3. Maximize your sales during the competition and provide your audience with a variety of your favorite product finds on TikTok shop!
4. Get rewards once the campaign period is over based on your live stream GMV (Gross Merchandise Value) sales ranking from all ecommerce content produced during this period
5. First come first serve competition
6. Only the GMV (Gross Merchandise Value) generated by SV and live streams hosted between the start date and end date of this challenge will be eligible for ranking on the leaderboard
